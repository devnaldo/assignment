01 — The project

A quieter kind of address, built around a garden

Verdant Grove is planned as a place to come home slowly. Six low-rise blocks wrap a central garden court, with the stilt level left open so air and light move freely between the towers.

Homes are arranged to catch morning light and evening cross breezes, and every living room opens onto a balcony deep enough to sit out on. Materially, the palette stays warm and restrained — beige stone, dark metal, and a great deal of green.
